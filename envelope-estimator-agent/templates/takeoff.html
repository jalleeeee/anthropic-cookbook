How the AI Takeoff Works
1
Sheet Identification — Claude Vision scans each page to find elevations, roof plans, sections, and schedules relevant to the envelope.
2
Scale Detection & Dimensions — Reads the drawing scale, extracts footprint, perimeter, wall heights, roof pitch, and all critical dimensions.
3

4
Window/Door Deductions — Extracts schedules and deducts openings from gross wall area for net siding quantities.
5
Waste & Pitch Adjustments — Applies trade-specific waste factors and roof pitch multipliers to get adjusted quantities.
6
4-Way Validation — A second AI model independently verifies quantities to catch errors. Variance flagged if >2%.
7
Cost Estimate & Proposal — Prices every line item via DDC-CWICR, generates a professional bid-ready proposal.
